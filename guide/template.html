Let Chrome install apps

The first time, Android asks whether Chrome may install apps. Tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, and go back.

Didn't see this screen?You already allowed it before. Go on to the next step.
5

Get past Google Play Protect

Play Protect blocks apps that don't come from the store and ask to read notifications. That's exactly what this app does, so the block is expected. Two ways through:

  1. The short way: in the block dialog, tap More details. If you see Install anyway, tap it and go to step 6.
  2. No "Install anyway"? Turn scanning off for a moment. Open Google Play, then profile picture→Play Protect→⚙️ Settings, turn off Scan apps with Play Protect and confirm Turn off. Go back to Downloads and open the file again.
Important: turn scanning back onOnce the app is installed, go back to the same Play Protect screen and turn scanning on again. The app keeps working with scanning on.
6

Install WA Forwarder

Tap Install. After a few seconds tap Open, or just go on to the next step.

"App not installed"?There is probably an older copy on the phone that was signed with a different key. Uninstall it and try again.
7

Set up the app in one tap

Go back to the setup page in Chrome (from step 2) and tap Set up the app automatically. The app opens with your server URL and key already filled in, and Connect to server shows a green ✓.

The button didn't open the app?In the app, tap Set up next to "Connect to server" (or Edit under "Connection details") and copy the Server URL and Personal key from the setup page by hand.

Now tap Allow next to step 2, Notification access.

8

Allow notification access (including "Restricted setting")

This is the permission that matters most: without it the app sees nothing. The Notification access screen opens. Turn on the switch for WA Forwarder.

On Android 13 and later, an app that didn't come from the store doesn't get this permission right away. The switch is greyed out and a Restricted setting dialog appears. That's expected. To unlock it:

  1. Tap OK on the "Restricted setting" dialog.
  2. Open Settings→Apps→WA Forwarder (or long-press the app icon, then ⓘ).
  3. Tap ⋮ in the top corner and choose Allow restricted settings. You may be asked for your fingerprint.
  4. Back in the app, tap Allow next to "Notification access" again, turn on the switch and confirm. The app shows this path as a reminder under the step.
No ⋮ on the screen?That happens when it's already unlocked, or on older Android versions. Just turn on the switch. Samsung and Xiaomi use slightly different names, but the path is the same.
9

Turn off battery optimization

Android likes to put background apps to sleep, and then messages stop flowing. In the app, tap Allow next to step 3, Run in background, and tap Allow in the dialog.

The app does nothing when no new messages arrive, so the battery impact is negligible.

Xiaomi, Samsung, HuaweiThese add an extra layer. See "Messages stop after a while" under Troubleshooting.

Something not working?

The common problems and what to do about each.

The "Notification access" switch is greyed out

That's Android 13+ "Restricted setting". Follow step 8: Settings → Apps → WA Forwarder → ⋮ → Allow restricted settings, then turn it on again.

No ⋮? Tap the greyed-out switch once first (that's what "arms" the menu option), then go back to App info.

Play Protect keeps blocking even with scanning off

Close Google Play completely, wait half a minute and open the install file again from Files → Downloads. Still blocked? Download the file again from the setup page.

Messages arrive, but stop after a few hours

The manufacturer's battery saver put the app to sleep. Check that "Run in background" shows ✓ in the app, and also:

Samsung: Settings → Battery → Background usage limits. Make sure WA Forwarder is not under "Sleeping apps" and add it to "Never sleeping apps".

Xiaomi / Redmi: App info → Autostart → on, and under Battery saver choose No restrictions.

Huawei / Honor: Settings → Battery → App launch → WA Forwarder → Manage manually, and turn on all three switches.

The last status shows an error

401: the key in the app is wrong, or a new link was issued. Open the latest setup link and tap "Set up the app automatically" again.

No internet / timeout: no harm done. Messages wait in a queue on the phone and are sent when the connection is back. You can tap "Retry stuck messages".

Some messages are missing or cut off

The app only sees what's in the notification. So nothing arrives from muted chats, from chats you had open on screen at the time, and no photos themselves (just "📷 Photo" and the caption). Very long messages may be truncated.

Claude can't find the tool, or the connector shows an error

In Settings › Connectors, check that the URL was pasted in full, with no trailing space. If you got a new setup link, replace the URL here too: delete the connector and add it again.

I lost the setup link / changed phones

{{LOST_LINK_ACTION}} The old link stops working, so repeat step 7 (on the phone) and step 11 (in Claude).

How do I pause or remove it?

To pause: turn off "Forwarding on" in the app. To remove: uninstall the app, delete the connector in Claude, and {{DELETE_ACTION}} Deleting the user also deletes all stored messages.

Privacy, no fine print

Worth reading before you install.

👀 No blue ticks

The app never opens WhatsApp, never taps the notification and never replies. As far as the sender knows, the message is still unread.

🔑 A key per person

Every user has separate keys for the app and for Claude. No user can see another user's messages, and the server stores only hashes of the keys.

🗓️ Deleted automatically

Messages are deleted from the server after {{RETENTION_DAYS}} days. You can also skip specific chats, or all groups, right in the app.
